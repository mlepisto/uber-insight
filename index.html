The board runs fully offline on a typical OGG timetable. To pull real delays / actual landings for today, paste a free AeroDataBox RapidAPI key (free tier, no card). It stays in your browser only. This layer is experimental.

How to read it. Each row is a 30-minute window. The bar and seat count are how many arriving seats hit the curb in that window — bigger bar, bigger wave of people needing a ride. Mainland and widebody arrivals are weighted heavier than interisland (visitors with bags and hotel trips beat locals on a short hop). Peak blocks are your best odds; stage before them, not during.

On the data. Flights are a realistic typical OGG weekday/weekend template — airlines, routes, aircraft and seat counts are real, but exact times and flight numbers drift, so treat it as a planning pattern, not a live tower feed. Verify big days against FlightAware. Turn on the live overlay above for actual delays/landings. Cruise calls are the recurring Kahului Harbor pattern (Pride of America overnights weekly) plus flagged examples — confirm dates on the Maui / Kahului Harbor cruise schedule before banking a cruise day.

Seats are per-aircraft estimates. All times Hawai‘i Standard Time (HST, no DST).
