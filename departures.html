How to read it. Each row is a 30-minute window, bucketed by when travelers likely leave for the airport — not their flight time. A mainland flight at 3:40pm puts its riders on the curb around 1:10pm (flight time minus the lead above). Interisland travelers leave an hour later than mainland. Bigger bar, bigger wave of people needing a ride toward OGG. Stage near the resorts (Wailea, Kā‘anapali, Kīhei) before a peak, not at the airport.

On the data. Departures come from the same daily airport feed as arrivals — no extra API cost. Days the feed hasn't been pulled yet fall back to a typical OGG departure timetable (real airlines, routes and aircraft, approximate times) — a planning pattern, not a promise. The lead-time is an assumption you can tune above; it's the single biggest lever on where these waves land.
